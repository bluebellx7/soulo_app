Soulo 1.1.1 refreshes browsing with a customizable toolbar and preview, current-screen and high-resolution scrolling captures, and 50%–200% page zoom. Captures can be saved, shared, and used with system Live Text. Full-screen and More menus now provide direct Home access, while independent frosted-glass controls keep the toolbar readable. Bookmarks, recent browsing, and downloads share one Library. Site information, Private Browsing, shake actions, on-demand camera and microphone permission, and iCloud settings sync were refined. We also fixed ad rules that could blank desktop pages, Library navigation, per-site ad controls, repeated shake actions, and video toolbar layout. UserScript compatibility is improved and remains Experimental.
