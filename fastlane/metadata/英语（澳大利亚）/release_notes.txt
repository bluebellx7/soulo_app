1.2.8

• New default player for local and BT videos, with episode selection, 0.5–4× speed and a system player option for compatible formats.
• Improved fullscreen controls: auto-hide, hold for temporary 2× speed, and swipe to adjust brightness on the left or volume on the right.
• Fixed audio-only playback in some HEVC/MKV videos and loading indicators that stayed visible during playback.
• Improved web popup and ad filtering compatibility to prevent incorrectly blocked windows and leftover video overlays.
• Fixed conflicts between Library swipe-to-delete and tab switching, aligned folders in both file views, and kept recent search items steady when held.
